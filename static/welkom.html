How it works

1 · Tell it

Type your dream, speak it, or talk it through with Vera, the guide who listens and asks.

2 · Choose

How you want it visualised — text only, five drawn panels, or with the key moment moving. And how you want it read: psychological, symbolic, spiritual, or let it choose.

3 · Watch it grow

Every dream counts in the next reading. Recurring places, people and animals tie themselves together, and your chakra pillar fills up night by night.

Where the limits are

Entertainment, not prediction

The look ahead is written to enjoy, not to act on. It is never about health, illness, money, pregnancy or anyone's death. Dreamverse is not care and not therapy.

Some dreams get help instead

A dream about violence or suicide still gets a careful reading, with the helplines for your country beside it. An explicitly sexual dream is not imagined at all, and costs you nothing.

Your dreams are yours

Download everything as a zip whenever you like, or have it all erased. We keep no password, only a hash. What we keep and who processes it is in the privacy statement.

The small print

Subscription and cancelling

Subscriptions renew monthly and you cancel them yourself in the app, at any moment. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month; there is no notice period. Tokens you already bought stay yours.

Refunds
