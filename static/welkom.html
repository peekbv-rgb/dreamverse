How it works

1 · Tell it

Type your dream, speak it, or talk it through with Vera, the guide who listens and asks.

2 · Choose

How you want it visualised — text only, five drawn panels, or with the key moment moving. And how you want it read: psychological, symbolic, spiritual, or let it choose.

3 · Watch it grow

Every dream counts in the next reading. Recurring places, people and animals tie themselves together, and your chakra pillar fills up night by night.

Why I built Dreamverse

I kept wondering why the same people, places and situations returned in dreams years apart. The problem was simple: by dream ten, dream three was already forgotten.

So I built something that would not forget.

— Ruud, founder of Dreamverse

Nobody else can open them

Your dreams, readings and panels sit under your account. Every request checks that what you are asking for is yours; another account asking for your panel gets nothing back.

No tracking, and no cookie banner

There is no advertising network here, no analytics script, and nothing that follows you to the next site. That is why you were not asked to accept anything: there is nothing to accept. Your dreams are never sold and never used for advertising.

Leaving takes one click

Download the lot as a zip whenever you like, or delete your account and everything in it. That is permanent, and it clears our active systems; our server keeps an automatic snapshot for seven days. The companies that help make a dream are named in the privacy statement, so you can see exactly who touches what.

Where the limits are

Entertainment, not prediction

The look ahead is written to enjoy, not to act on. It is never about health, illness, money, pregnancy or anyone's death. Dreamverse is not care and not therapy.

Some dreams get help instead

A dream about violence or suicide still gets a careful reading, with the helplines for your country beside it. An explicitly sexual dream is not imagined at all, and costs you nothing.

Your dreams are yours

Download everything as a zip whenever you like, or have it all erased. We keep no password, only a hash. What we keep and who processes it is in the privacy statement.
